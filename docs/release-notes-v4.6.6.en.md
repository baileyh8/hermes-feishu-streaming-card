# V4.6.6 — Approval, tool and notice cleanup

This release consolidates the remaining #337/#340 and PR #338/#339 experience fixes while retaining the V4.6.x version line and reading defaults.

- Compact duplicate decided approval blocks only after the turn ends and an explicit PATCH confirms the separate full question, scope and decision receipt. Keep pending, sole-owner, oversized or failed-delivery records. Consecutive approvals are checked separately; clarify is unchanged. In-memory display proof never restores execution or authorization.
- Reserve bounded timeline slots for running tools, their immediate predecessors and their owning reasoning, fixing older #24/#25 calls disappearing behind newer activity. Body and panel ordinals agree; unfinished tools display as interrupted after the turn ends.
- Owned restart, online, shutdown and drain notices expire after 15 seconds. Confirmed delivery in the exact profile/bot/chat/thread can wake the timer early. Home uses its own deadline; other topics cannot retire it early. Private persistence restores deadlines and failed-delete cooldowns.
- Known native one-line background success notices expire after 15 seconds. Preserve failures, output-bearing results and complete approval receipts. Native expired-approval corrections remain: they may be the only record that no command ran after a callback displayed Approved. Exact producer, adapter, profile and application evidence is required; ordinary quoted text has no recall authority. The native requester restart message uses the colored ♻️ without changing its meaning.
- Terminal tool compaction hides successful tools while retaining failed, cancelled and interrupted work, including unsuccessful calls on otherwise completed turns. Defaults remain `hide_completed_tool_activity: false`, `timeline_order: newest_first` and `timeline_tools_per_reasoning: 0`.

- After an ambiguous interaction POST failure, briefly poll only the read endpoint for a late card instead of replaying the event or prematurely emitting native fallback. The 3-second grace is capped at 5 seconds, with each lookup and sleep constrained by the remaining budget; explicit rejection still falls back immediately. The original authored commit from [tidytorch](https://github.com/tidytorch) in [PR #342](https://github.com/baileyh8/hermes-feishu-streaming-card/pull/342) is retained and strengthened with a real lost-response HTTP regression.

See [notice lifecycle](wiki/notice-lifecycle.md), [approval continuation](wiki/interaction-continuation.md), [reading settings](wiki/reading-presets.md) and [acceptance](wiki/feishu-acceptance-v4.6.6.md). Unknown Hermes signatures, unproven historical messages and platform recall refusals preserve messages. No atomic cross-system delivery promise or AMD/9Router, model or Hermes-core change is implied.
